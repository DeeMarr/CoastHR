## Who we are

Our website address is: https://coasthr.com.au.

## Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your message. This is sent to us via our form provider (Formspree) and used only to respond to your enquiry.

This website does not use visitor accounts, comments, or user logins, and does not collect payment or financial information.

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics. If that changes in future (for example, adding Google Analytics), this policy will be updated to reflect it.

## Embedded content

Some pages include embedded video. Depending on how the video is hosted, the provider may collect standard technical data (such as IP address) when the video is viewed, in the same way as visiting any other website.

## How long we keep your data

Contact form enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and for reasonable business record-keeping, and are not shared with third parties other than our form provider (Formspree), which processes the submission on our behalf.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, and request that we correct or delete it, by contacting us at {{ site.email }}.
