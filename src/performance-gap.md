### Grow Performance

Everyone in your team knows what they need to do. Right? Or do they?

Your business has changed since they started. Are they still doing what you needed back then, or what you actually need from them now?

And when did you last sit down and tell them how they're going?

When your team's day-to-day work lines up with where the business is headed, everyone knows what a good job looks like, and what their part is in getting there. That makes it easier for them to do good work, and easier for you to spot it when someone's drifted off track.

CoastHR helps you break your business goals down into plain terms, and talk to your team about what that looks like in their job. Plain English. No corporate jargon. Just a conversation that helps each person see how their work helps your clients, your team, and the business.

And when that conversation shows someone's genuinely off track, that's what the Performance Gap Framework is for.
