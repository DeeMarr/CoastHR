## Taking on an apprentice?

Apprenticeships come with their own set of employment rules — probation periods, training agreements, and Award conditions that don't work quite like standard employment. Getting it wrong can be costly.

CoastHR helps small business employers get apprentice employment right from day one: contracts, onboarding, and ongoing compliance with the relevant training authority.
