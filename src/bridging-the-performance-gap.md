### Not every performance problem looks the same

After years of working with small and growing businesses, one thing is clear: performance problems don't come from one place. They come from five.

We call them the five factors, and every performance issue your team has right now traces back to at least one of them.

**Skill**

Does your team member actually have the capability to do what you're asking? Skill isn't just about qualifications. It's built through good induction, structured training, coaching and deliberate practice. When it's missing, performance suffers, no matter how much goodwill is in the room.

**Knowledge**

Does your team member know what's expected of them? This sounds basic, but a lack of role clarity is one of the most common, and most preventable, causes of underperformance. Unclear expectations aren't just a management problem. They're a recognised psychosocial hazard that businesses need to manage.

**Confidence**

Does your team member believe they can do it? Confidence is shaped by the environment around a person. Regular feedback, genuine recognition, and a leadership style that trusts rather than micromanages, these are what build a team member's belief in their own ability. Without them, even skilled, knowledgeable people hold back.

**Motivation**

Does your team member have a reason to bring their best effort? Purpose, meaningful work, fair conditions, growth opportunities and wellbeing support all play a role here. When motivation is missing, capability doesn't translate into performance, and no amount of pressure will change that.

**The Enabling Environment**

Is your workplace set up for success? The four factors above all depend on a foundation of fair, well-managed, well-resourced work. Unreasonable workloads, poor leadership, inadequate tools and unclear processes will undermine even the best team members.

Once you know where the gap is, everything changes. You stop having the same conversations. You start having the right ones.
