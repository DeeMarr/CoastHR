##### Deanne founded CoastHR to help small and medium business owners, office managers and practice managers get their teams' performance on track.

For more than twenty years, Deanne held senior HR leadership roles, working closely with leaders, managers and employees to achieve business outcomes through developing and implementing targeted people initiatives across the full range of HR functions.

In 2018, Deanne relocated to the Whitsundays to assist with her partner's 50-person tourism business.

The global pandemic hurled standard business practices out the window, and she was thrown into managing the administration, crew, logistics, and compliance, and day-to-day vessel operations, for the 24/7/365 business.

Deanne helps small business owners, office managers and practice managers to get their teams performing. With extensive expertise in people, teams and processes, Deanne brings practical lived experience as a business owner, and a pragmatic, personal approach.

Now loving life on the Gold Coast, Deanne is a busy dog-mum, a keen cook and an avid gardener.
