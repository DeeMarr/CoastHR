## HR by Guesswork

Managing people is critical to your organisation's success, but without HR expertise, you're left to cobble together policies and procedures from AI tools, online articles, or forums — a dangerous practice. The result?

- **Wasted hours** hunting for templates and scripts.
- **Second-guessing** decisions with compliance risks looming.
- **No one to ask** when a tricky HR situation arises.

You deserve better. The business deserves better.

## The Hidden Cost of "Getting By" in HR

When you're not trained in HR and rely on AI, online templates, or guesswork, you're leaving your organisation exposed to serious risks — risks that may not be obvious now, but can have costly consequences down the track. Without proper HR processes in place, you could face:

- **Compliance breaches** — leading to fines, penalties, or legal action.
- **Unfair dismissal claims** — without correct documentation and processes, you could be vulnerable.
- **Employee grievances** — poor handling of issues can escalate quickly and damage team culture.
- **Wasted time and resources** — spending hours fixing problems that could have been prevented.
- **Reputation damage** — one poorly handled HR issue can affect your business's standing in the community or industry.

> Good intentions aren't enough in HR — you need the right systems, knowledge, and support.
